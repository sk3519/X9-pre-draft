( 02 ) House rules

Six things we don't negotiate.

/01

You meet everyone

The four on your first call are the four doing your work. No B-team appears after you sign.

/02

Honest dashboards

You see what we see — including the tests that flopped. Numbers over narratives.

/03

Fixed quotes

One number agreed before anything starts. Scope changes get re-quoted openly, never quietly absorbed.

/04

Weekly builds

You click real progress every week. Demos include the embarrassing parts — that's where trust lives.

/05

Fewer, better clients

We take projects we believe we can move. If yours isn't our shape, we'll say so and point you somewhere better.

/06

Cake on launch day

Every launch gets cake. Non-negotiable morale infrastructure since day one.
